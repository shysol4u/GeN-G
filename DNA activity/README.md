# DNA Detectives and the Mystery of Sick Plants

On November 18, 2026, our GeN-G team joined Mrs. Ching’s class at Camelot Intermediate School for an Educator for a Day activity. Students examined soybean and barley plants, explored white mold and cereal stem rust, built DNA sequences with LEGO bricks, and used a Brickopore model and Monster Maker to investigate imaginary plant pathogens. 3D printed plant cells, a DNA tower, and a painted nanopore model helped connect the whole plant to its cells and DNA.
