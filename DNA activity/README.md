# Educator for a Day- DNA Detectives and the Mystery of Sick Plants

On November 18, 2026, our GeN-G team joined Mrs. Ching’s class at Camelot Intermediate School (Brookings, South Dakota) for an Educator for a Day activity. 25 4th-grade students examined soybean and barley plants, explored white mold and cereal stem rust disease, visualized green fluorescence-tagged bacteria, and learned how  DNA codes information as a building block of life.  In our hands-on activity, these kids built DNA sequences with LEGO bricks, and used a Brickopore model and Monster Maker to investigate imaginary plant pathogens. 3D-printed plant cells, 3D-printed bacterial slides, a DNA tower, and a 3D-printed nanopore model, helped connect the educational story to cells and DNA.
